1. Launch an underwater world: ros2 launch uuv_gazebo_worlds auv_underwater_world.launch.py

2. Upload x300: ros2 launch x300_description upload_x300_default.launch.py

3. Launch a controller: 
	ros2 launch uuv_trajectory_control rov_sf_controller.launch.py uuv_name:=x300
	ros2 launch uuv_trajectory_control rov_ua_pid_controller.launch.py uuv_name:=x300
	ros2 launch uuv_trajectory_control rov_pid_controller.launch.py uuv_name:=x300
	ros2 launch x300_description x300_pid_controller.launch.py
	
4. Send waypoints: ros2 launch uuv_control_utils send_waypoints_file.launch.py uuv_name:=x300
