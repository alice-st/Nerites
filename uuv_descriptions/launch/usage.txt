Usage:
1. Launch a gz world:

ros2 launch uuv_gazebo_worlds auv_underwater_world.launch.py

ros2 launch uuv_descriptions upload_rexrov_default.launch.py
ros2 launch uuv_descriptions upload_rexrov.launch.py
ros2 launch uuv_descriptions upload_rexrov_default_noisy_pose.launch.py
