1. Launch an underwater world: ros2 launch uuv_gazebo_worlds auv_underwater_world.launch.py

2. Upload x300: ros2 launch eca_a9_description upload_eca_a9_default.launch.py

3. Launch a controller: 
	ros2 launch eca_a9_control start_geometric_tracking_control.launch.py
	ros2 launch eca_a9_control start_nmb_sm_control.launch.py

4. Send waypoints: ros2 launch uuv_control_utils send_waypoints_file.launch.py uuv_name:=x300
