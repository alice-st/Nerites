Usage:
1. Launch a gz world:

ros2 launch uuv_gazebo_worlds auv_underwater_world.launch.py

2. Launch a vehicle:

ros2 launch uuv_descriptions upload_rexrov_oberon4.launch.py


3. Launch the controller

ros2 launch oberon4_control joint_control.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov


4. poke it: 

ros2 topic pub /oberon4_arm_controller/commands std_msgs/msg/Float64MultiArray "
layout:
  dim: []
  data_offset: 0
data: [0.8, 0.5, -0.3, 0.2, -0.2]
" -1
publisher: beginning loop
publishing #1: std_msgs.msg.Float64MultiArray(layout=std_msgs.msg.MultiArrayLayout(dim=[], data_offset=0), data=[0.8, 0.5, -0.3, 0.2, -0.2])
