Usage:
1. Launch a gz world:

ros2 launch uuv_gazebo_worlds auv_underwater_world.launch.py

2. Launch a vehicle:

ros2 launch uuv_descriptions upload_rexrov_oberon7.launch.py


3. Launch the controller

ros2 launch oberon4_control joint_control.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov

4. poke it:

ros2 topic pub /oberon7_arm_controller/commands std_msgs/msg/Float64MultiArray "
layout:
  dim: []
  data_offset: 0
data: [1.0, 0.3, -0.5, 0.0, 0.2, 1.0]
" -1
