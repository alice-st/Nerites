Usage:
1. Launch a gz world:

ros2 launch uuv_gazebo_worlds auv_underwater_world.launch.py

2. Launch a vehicle:

ros2 launch uuv_descriptions upload_rexrov_oberon7.launch.py


3. Launch the controller

ros2 launch oberon7_control gripper_controllers.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov
ros2 launch oberon7_control joint_control.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov
ros2 launch oberon7_control joint_effort_controllers.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov
ros2 launch oberon7_control jt_cartesian_controller.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov
