Controller demos:

1. Start a controller:

ros2 launch uuv_gazebo start_mb_smc_demo.launch.py
ros2 launch uuv_gazebo start_nl_pid_demo.launch.py
ros2 launch uuv_gazebo start_nmb_smc_demo.launch.py
ros2 launch uuv_gazebo record_demo.launch.py
ros2 launch uuv_gazebo start_nmb_smc_demo_with_teleop.launch.py --to revisit
ros2 launch uuv_gazebo start_pid_demo_with_teleop.launch.py --to revisit

2. Send waypoints:

ros2 launch uuv_control_utils send_waypoints_file.launch uuv_name:=rexrov

3. Add disturbances:

ros2 launch uuv_gazebo start_disturbance_demo.launch.py uuv_name:=rexrov
